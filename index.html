Personal Social Scheduler

A self-hosted instance of Postiz for scheduling personal short-form content across social platforms.

What this is

  • Single-user deployment, not a public service
  • Schedules and publishes content to my own connected social accounts
  • Runs locally via Docker; no third-party data is collected from visitors
